# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial public release.

- Lists AVDs in a toolbar dropdown and launches the selected one headless.
- Streams the screen as H.264 at ~30fps via `screenrecord`, decoded with WebCodecs.
- Mouse input: click to tap, drag to swipe, scroll wheel to scroll.
- Keyboard input, including special keys and Ctrl/Alt/Shift combinations.
- Hardware Back / Home / Recents buttons on a draggable floating toolbar.
- Rotate left and right, including on apps that pin their own orientation.
- Full-resolution screenshot capture.
- Extended controls panel: battery level, charger and health; cellular network type,
  status and signal strength; GPS location; incoming calls and SMS; fingerprint sensor.
- Fit-to-height, fit-to-width, and 25%–300% zoom controls.
- Boot progress loader with stepped phases.
